## 2. Deep Frontier Vectors (January - September 2026)

### A. Biology & Genomic AI
- **Generative Protein Engineering (ESM3, AlphaFold 3, RFdiffusion):** Moving beyond predicting protein folds to *de novo* design of enzymes that degrade plastic, target specific cancer epitopes, and synthesize novel biomolecules.
- **Genomic Foundational Models (AlphaGenome / Ensembl Transformers):** Predicting the downstream regulatory impact of non-coding single nucleotide variants (SNVs) on RNA expression, chromatin accessibility, and splicing.

### B. Precision Agriculture & Robotics (Hardware + Software)
- **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models:** Models like OpenVLA and RT-2 evolved into real-time sub-10W edge robotics architectures.
- **Multi-Spectral Sensor Fusion:** Integrating thermal, LiDAR, and hyperspectral imaging directly with lightweight neural networks on embedded Nvidia Jetson / Raspberry Pi / ARM chips for millimeter-level robotic crop treatment and yield optimization.

### C. Test-Time Compute & Agentic Reasoning
- **Inference Scaling Laws:** Shifting compute from pre-training to test-time search (Tree-of-Thoughts, Monte Carlo Tree Search, self-correction loops).
- **Small Language Models (SLMs) on Edge Devices:** 1B–3B parameter quantized models (4-bit / 2-bit AWQ, GGUF) running locally on edge hardware with zero cloud latency.

## 3. The 15-Year-Old Founder's Roadmap to Physical AI Mastery
To build hardware + software + bio/agri systems by age 21 (2031):
1. **Layer 0 (Math & Data Structures):** Linear algebra (tensors, matrices), multivariable calculus (gradients), graph theory, search trees.
2. **Layer 1 (Systems & Hardware):** C/C++ and Rust. Understanding pointers, UART/SPI/I2C sensor protocols, microcontrollers (ESP32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, Jetson Nano).
3. **Layer 2 (Neural Foundations):** Building CNNs, Transformers, and VLA policies from raw tensors without high-level black-box abstractions.
4. **Layer 3 (Domain Integration):** Interfacing software models with physical actuators, cameras, biological sequence databases (NCBI/UniProt), and edge sensors.
